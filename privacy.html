隐私政策（中文）

一句话总结：NetKeeper 上网管家不收集、不上传、不出售任何用户数据。应用没有服务器端组件，没有账号系统，所有数据只保存在您的电脑本地。

本隐私政策适用于由开发者 stren.chen 发布、通过 Microsoft Store 分发的 Windows 桌面应用「NetKeeper 上网管家」（下称"本应用"）。请您在使用前仔细阅读。

1. 我们收集的信息

无。本应用：

  • 不需要注册、不需要登录、没有账号体系；
  • 不包含任何遥测、统计分析、崩溃上报或广告 SDK；
  • 不嵌入任何第三方数据收集组件；
  • 没有属于开发者的服务器，技术上不具备"把您的数据传给我们"的通道。

2. 存储在您设备上的数据

为了让功能可用（记住您的设置、站点列表、书签和历史记录），本应用会在您电脑的 %AppData%\NetKeeper\ 目录下保存以下本地 JSON 文件：

  • settings.json —— 应用设置（监控间隔、测速节点、是否自启等）；
  • sites.json —— 您维护的连通性检测站点列表；
  • bookmarks.json —— 您的网址书签；
  • check-history.json —— 连通性检测历史（最多 2000 条，超出自动淘汰最旧记录）；
  • speed-history.json —— 测速历史（最多 200 条）。

这些数据只存在于您的设备上，不会被上传。您可以随时在应用内导出（CSV/TXT，需您手动触发）或直接删除上述文件夹以清除全部数据；卸载应用后删除该文件夹即不留任何痕迹。

3. 网络访问说明（重要）

本应用是一款网络诊断工具，其网络连接全部由您主动发起的功能产生，且直接发生在您的设备与目标服务之间：

  • 连通性检测：向您列表中的站点/地址发送 ICMP Ping、TCP 端口连接或 HTTP(S) 请求（例如 baidu.com、google.com 等，列表完全由您管理）。目标网站会像收到任何普通网络访问一样看到您的 IP 地址，其数据处理遵循对方自己的隐私政策，与开发者无关，我们也接收不到任何此类数据。
  • 网络测速：默认连接 Cloudflare 的公共测速服务 speed.cloudflare.com 进行下载/上传测试（测速节点可在设置中自行更改）。测速过程中传输的是测试用的随机数据块，不包含任何个人信息。
  • 代理：HTTP 检测与测速遵循您系统当前的代理设置。

除上述由您主动触发的连接外，本应用不会在后台向任何第三方发送任何信息。

4. 本机系统信息

为显示网络状态，本应用会读取您本机网卡的名称、类型（Wi-Fi/以太网）、本机 IPv4 地址、网关与 DNS 服务器地址。这些信息仅用于界面显示，不会写入报告、不会上传。

5. 开机自启动

仅当您在设置中主动开启"开机自启"时，本应用才会在当前用户的注册表 HKCU\...\CurrentVersion\Run 下写入一个启动项（值名 NetKeeper）。您可以随时在设置中关闭，关闭后该启动项立即被移除。

6. 权限说明

  • internetClient：访问网络，用于连通性检测与测速；
  • runFullTrust：Win32 桌面应用能力，用于执行原始 ICMP/TCP 检测、调用系统网络接口 API（iphlpapi）以及系统托盘常驻。

以上权限均服务于产品核心功能，不用于任何形式的数据收集。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何用户数据，因此也不存在收集儿童个人信息的情形。所有年龄段均可放心使用。

8. 数据安全

您的所有数据都留在您自己的设备上，由 Windows 的用户账户权限体系保护。由于不存在数据上传，也不存在传输途中泄露的问题。

9. 政策变更

如本政策发生修订，我们会在本页面发布新版本并更新"生效日期"。重大变更会以显著方式在页面顶部标注。

10. 联系我们

Privacy Policy (English)

In one sentence: NetKeeper collects nothing, uploads nothing and sells nothing. The app has no server-side component and no accounts; every piece of data stays on your own PC.

This privacy policy applies to the Windows desktop application NetKeeper ("the App"), published by developer stren.chen and distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. Information We Collect

None. The App:

  • requires no sign-up, no login and has no account system;
  • contains no telemetry, analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • embeds no third-party data-collection components;
  • has no developer-owned server, so there is technically no channel through which your data could reach us.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To make its features work (remembering your settings, site list, bookmarks and history), the App stores the following local JSON files under %AppData%\NetKeeper\ on your PC:

  • settings.json — app settings (monitor interval, speed-test endpoints, autostart, etc.);
  • sites.json — your list of connectivity-check targets;
  • bookmarks.json — your web bookmarks;
  • check-history.json — connectivity-check history (capped at 2,000 records, oldest dropped first);
  • speed-history.json — speed-test history (capped at 200 records).

This data never leaves your device. You may export it at any time (CSV/TXT, always user-initiated) or simply delete the folder above to erase everything. Uninstalling the App and deleting that folder leaves no trace.

3. Network Access (Important)

The App is a network diagnostics tool. All of its network connections are produced by features you actively trigger, and they run directly between your device and the target service:

  • Connectivity checks: ICMP ping, TCP port connect or HTTP(S) requests to the sites/addresses in your list (e.g. baidu.com, google.com — the list is entirely under your control). Target websites see your IP address exactly as they would for any ordinary visit; their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies. The developer is not involved and receives none of it.
  • Speed test: by default the App connects to Cloudflare's public speed-test service speed.cloudflare.com (endpoints are configurable in Settings). Only random test payloads are transferred — no personal information.
  • Proxy: HTTP checks and speed tests honor your current system proxy configuration.

Apart from the connections you initiate as described above, the App never sends anything to us or to any third party in the background.

4. Local System Information

To display your network status, the App reads your network adapter's name, type (Wi-Fi/Ethernet), local IPv4 address, gateway and DNS server addresses. This information is shown in the UI only — it is not recorded into reports and not uploaded.

5. Autostart

Only if you explicitly enable "Start with Windows" in Settings does the App write a startup entry (value name NetKeeper) under the current user's registry key HKCU\...\CurrentVersion\Run. You can disable it in Settings at any time, which removes the entry immediately.

6. Permissions

  • internetClient — network access, used for connectivity checks and speed tests;
  • runFullTrust — Win32 desktop capability, used for raw ICMP/TCP probing, system network-interface APIs (iphlpapi) and system-tray residency.

These permissions serve the product's core functionality only and are never used to collect data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App collects no user data whatsoever, so no personal information from children (or anyone else) is ever collected. It is safe for all ages.

8. Data Security

All of your data remains on your own device, protected by your Windows user-account permissions. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no risk of interception in transit.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is revised, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top of the page.
